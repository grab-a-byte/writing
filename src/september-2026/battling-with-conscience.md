## Toxicity Was Your Favourite Song

Danny had just finished his set when he met Nina. She was awed by his music and invited him for a drink where she asked all sorts of questions but the one that sealed the deal was, “What do you do for a job?”. He’d mentioned he was a songwriter and music producer and she’d lit up in such a way that Danny had fallen for her there and then. The romance bloomed for a few months until Nina learned of the reality of what Danny did. She’d imagined a big studio with musicians in and out all the time; the reality was Danny made enough to pay rent, buy food and worked from a table in his kitchen. So, it all blew apart, Nina left him calling him a fraud and liar, Danny’s heart was broken as he hadn’t realised she was only after fame and fortune. This however started a chain of events nobody saw coming.

To work through his misery, Danny did what he always did and wrote a song about their relationship. He worked on it for days before deciding it was done. He trimmed out a sample, posted it on his website with a note of “sample for showcase purposes, full song not available”. The song had helped him process his feelings, but he didn’t want to ruin Nina’s life. With that he grabbed some beers, and drank himself asleep.

First order the next day, checking emails. He found the usual slurry of spam email, people offering to pay him in exposure, and one that simply said “Name your price for the exclusive rights on the full version of ‘Toxicity was your favourite song’?”. He assumed it was more spam but jokingly replied “£1,000,000”.\
The response was instant, “Deal, here’s the contract.”\
Danny was seconds away from signing it before he stopped himself. There was a reason he had decided to not publish the full song. He hadn’t wanted all the obvious jabs at the relationship to be public. He hadn’t wanted to risk ruining Nina’s life. But, a million pounds was one million pounds. Could he really sell his morals for money? The longer he sat with it, the more opposition to each side he thought about.\
Do I make my songs for my own feeling and expression, or am I actually trying to be rich and famous?\
If I sell it, is it a betrayal of the relationship, or is it already gone and he doesn’t need to protect the memory of it.\
Does selling it make him a bad person, someone who doesn’t care or is it simply a matter of being able to ensure he succeeds and doesn't have to worry about rent next month?\
Art or Profit.\
Feeling vs Reality.\
Nina vs Him.\
The turmoil stopped when the next email came in.\
“The previous contract has expired. The new offer is £750,000”. £250,000 lost for a few hours deliberating. Within the next few minutes, the contract was signed. Simultaneously, he felt relief and fear. His heart had been laid out in song and now the world was going to hear it. Well at least on some version of it after the producers had put their spin on it. The deal was done and everything was sent, he sat there continuing to deliberate if he’d made the right choice with a slight tinge of regret.

A few weeks later in the local bar, a song came on. He recognised all the lyrics, he’d written them. The song had made number 2 in the charts. He was enjoying his beer when he saw Nina walking towards him. She stopped just in front of him. He didn’t see the next part, only felt it as her hand connected in an almighty slap that silenced the bar.
“I scammed my granny eh?!” Nina said with fury, clearly referencing his lyrics. Next she kissed him and asked “So when are we going to Miami for the awards?”. He knew then he’d made the right choice for himself. He downed his pint, stared her in the face and replied\
“We aren’t. I am.”\
He heard the jitter of people in the background and began to wonder if he’d been too harsh. His conscience was clear about the song but it might not be for this. He hadn’t stopped to consider the fact that he didn’t have to worry about her life anymore, he had to worry about what damage he might have done to his own.
